About this Event

This is more than just another meetup.

It’s the Indian launch of a global movement to empower people to speak up. It’s about APIs and the tech, the platforms, the AI and the business models they enable. It’s about breaking the silence and empowering underrepresented voices in this ecosystem—especially women.

This is for people needing a nudge to take their next step with speaking in public. Whether you’re wanting to take the first small step…or you’re levelling up your speaking career…or you’re sponsoring others to do so.

Introducing the inaugural event in India of our popular in-person GET /Connecting meet-up series, ahead of apidays India 2025, designed to build confidence, provide support, and foster community.

Why Join?

  • You’ll meet like-minded individuals in a friendly environment.
  • You’ll make new connections with other conference participants: attendees, speakers and panelists.
  • Learn confidence-building strategies to overcome the fear of public speaking.
  • Get tips on submitting a CFP (Call for Proposal) for tech conferences.
  • Other attendees include fellow WIA members and allies from the wider community of API professionals and partners.
Specmatic is delighted to organise this Women in APIs event in partnership with APIsFirst and apidays and hosted by Zeta. They’ll be represented to support your networking opportunities and share what they are doing to help people be seen and heard in the public domain.

Who Should Attend?

Everyone is welcome to our GET /Connecting meetups, regardless of gender. This meetup will be particularly important for tech and business professionals working in and around APIs who are also:
  • First-time or aspiring tech speakers
  • Engineers, designers, and product folks curious about getting involved in conferences
  • Managers and team leaders looking to support their team’s visibility and growth
